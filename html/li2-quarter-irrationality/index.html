Irrationality of the dilogarithm at 1/4

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
Abstract.

We prove that Li2⁡(1/4)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(1/4) is irrational. Irrationality of Li2⁡(1/s)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(1/s) was known for integers s≤−5s\leq-5 and s≥6s\geq 6 by work of Hata and of Rhin and Viola, and Prellberg recently proved it for s=−4,5,−3s=-4,5,-3. The linear forms in 11 and Li2⁡(1/4)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(1/4) come from one double integral of Viola and Zudilin at z=4z=4. We expand it into Rhin–Viola integrals in three ways and apply their factorial transformations to each term. This gives a common factor of the coefficients of the NNth form. The third expansion adds the primes between 265⁢N265N and 322⁢N322N to this factor, which is too small without them. We bound the integral by the weighted arithmetic-geometric mean inequality and by concavity on circular arcs.
