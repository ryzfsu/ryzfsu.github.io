The Goldbach exceptional set: an exponent 11/20

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
Abstract.

We prove E⁢(X)≪εX11/20+εE(X)\ll_{\varepsilon}X^{11/20+\varepsilon} for every ε>0\varepsilon>0. Here E⁢(X)E(X) counts the positive even integers at most XX that are not sums of two primes. A four-level Harman decomposition gives a signed prime minorant. Exact rational bounds for four discarded families give a positive lower bound for its logarithmically weighted mass. We prove a Bombieri–Vinogradov estimate at level X9/20−ηX^{9/20-\eta}, with both maxima and a divisor weight. We also bound the exponential sum of the same weight on the minor arcs. The major arcs use classical zero-density estimates and the zero-free half-plane ℜ⁡s>7/8\Re s>7/8 of OpenAI [6, Theorem 1.1].

Key words and phrases:
Goldbach problem, exceptional set, Harman sieve, Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem, Dirichlet LL-functions
