Irrationality of the dilogarithm at 1/2

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
Abstract.

We prove that Li2⁡(1/2)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(1/2) is irrational. Irrationality of Li2⁡(1/s)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(1/s) was known for integers s≤−5s\leq-5 and s≥6s\geq 6 by work of Hata and of Rhin and Viola, and Prellberg recently proved it for s=−4,5,−3s=-4,5,-3. The approximations come from determinants of moments of 1/(x⁢y−2)1/(xy-2), differentiated m/2m/2 times in the parameter. We bound their values by a logarithmic energy on an ellipse, certified by directed interval arithmetic. Valuations of binomial coefficients give a common denominator of the coefficients. A Lucas congruence modulo 8383 gives nonvanishing along m=32⋅83km=32\cdot 83^{k}. Three finite computations are part of the proof.
