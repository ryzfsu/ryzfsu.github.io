Irrationality of the trilogarithm at reciprocals of integers of absolute value at least 27

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
Abstract.

We prove that Li3⁡(1/a)\operatorname{Li}_{3}(1/a) is irrational for every integer a≥27a\geq 27 and every integer a≤−27a\leq-27. One determinant construction is used on both sides. The positive side takes m=12⁢Qm=12Q and g=4⁢Qg=4Q; the negative side takes m=16⁢Qm=16Q and g=5⁢Qg=5Q. We prove a conservative coefficientwise integerizer, nonvanishing along prime-power towers, and a logarithmic-energy estimate. On the negative side the estimate rests on a signed residue identity along a bent contour. Exact rational and directed interval certificates give four strict endpoint margins. Slope comparisons carry them along both rays.
