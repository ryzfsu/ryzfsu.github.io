Irrationality of the trilogarithm at reciprocals of integers of absolute value at least 27

Ruiyi Zhang · Preprint, 2026-10-15 · 33 pages

PDFDOI 10.5281/zenodo.23395794Source and checks

Abstract

We prove that Li_3(1/a) is irrational for every integer a ≥ 27 and every integer a ≤ −27. One determinant construction is used on both sides. The positive side takes m = 12Q and g = 4Q; the negative side takes m = 16Q and g = 5Q. We prove a conservative coefficientwise integerizer, nonvanishing along prime-power towers, and a logarithmic-energy estimate. On the negative side the estimate rests on a signed residue identity along a bent contour. Exact rational and directed interval certificates give four strict endpoint margins. Slope comparisons carry them along both rays.
