Irrationality of the dilogarithm at −1/2-1/2

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
Abstract.

We prove that Li2⁡(−1/2)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(-1/2) is irrational. Irrationality of Li2⁡(1/s)\operatorname{Li}_{2}(1/s) was known for integers s≤−5s\leq-5 and s≥6s\geq 6 by work of Hata and of Rhin and Viola, and Prellberg recently proved it for s=−4,5,−3s=-4,5,-3. We define polynomials Gm⁢(T)G_{m}(T) with rational coefficients by an interpolation determinant. The rank of this determinant modulo primes pp with m/5<p<5⁢m/4m/5<p<5m/4 bounds the denominators of the coefficients. A concavity inequality for a logarithmic energy bounds Gm⁢(Li2⁡(−1/2))G_{m}(\operatorname{Li}_{2}(-1/2)). The only interval-arithmetic step is the potential certificate. The exact data in Table A.1, the coefficients of G4G_{4}, and the 9×99\times 9 determinant modulo 1111 are printed and hand-checkable. A congruence modulo 1111 shows that GmG_{m} has no rational zero for m=4⋅11tm=4\cdot 11^{t}.
