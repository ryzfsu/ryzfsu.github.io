A primitive-ratio cut and a 7/127/12 bound for the a⁢b⁢cabc exceptional set

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
Abstract.

We prove N1⁢(X)≪εX7/12+εN_{1}(X)\ll_{\varepsilon}X^{7/12+\varepsilon}, where Nλ⁢(X)N_{\lambda}(X) counts the a⁢b⁢cabc triples of exponent λ\lambda in [1,X]3[1,X]^{3}. The previous bound proved in full was X13/20+εX^{13/20+\varepsilon}, and Bernert (arXiv:2506.13364, Section 4) sketched X8/13+εX^{8/13+\varepsilon}. We keep the anatomic reduction of Bernert, Browning, Lichtman and Teräväinen. It gives 2222 inequalities for the exponents of a box. We add two exact rows per side for the root classes of index at least seven, and a cut. The cut fixes all but two coprime products on two sides. The remaining pairs are primitive points of a lattice. Its determinant is a product of roots on the third side. This gives 1 560 1951\,560\,195 further inequalities. An exact Farkas certificate over 81928192 cells and 10 82010\,820 leaves gives exponent 7/127/12 for the box count. On each leaf the multipliers sum to at most 18/718/7. This pays for the losses at λ=1\lambda=1.
