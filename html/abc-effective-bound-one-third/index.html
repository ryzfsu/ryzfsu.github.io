Improving the Stewart–Yu bound for all abc triples

Ruiyi Zhang Independent Researcher, Columbia, Maryland, USA
(Date: November 13, 2026)
Abstract.

We prove that log⁡c<A⁢R1/3⁢exp⁡(−log⁡R/(3000⁢log⁡log⁡R))\log c<AR^{1/3}\exp(-\log R/(3000\log\log R)) for coprime positive integers a+b=ca+b=c, where R=rad⁡(a⁢b⁢c)R=\operatorname{rad}(abc) and AA is absolute and effectively computable. The proof combines estimates at maximal prime powers with a growing number of auxiliary primes in a rational logarithmic form.

Key words and phrases:
abc conjecture, radical, effective bounds, pp-adic logarithmic forms

Primary 11D75; Secondary 11J86, 11D61

1. Introduction

For positive coprime integers a+b=ca+b=c, put R=rad⁡(a⁢b⁢c)R=\operatorname{rad}(abc). The a⁢b⁢cabc conjecture predicts c<Kε⁢R1+εc<K_{\varepsilon}R^{1+\varepsilon} for every ε>0\varepsilon>0. It remains open. Stewart–Yu [5, p. 226] proved log⁡c<R2/3+C/log⁡log⁡R\log c<R^{2/3+C/\log\log R} for c>2c>2. Their later bound [6, Theorem 1, p. 170] is log⁡c≪R1/3⁢(log⁡R)3\log c\ll R^{1/3}(\log R)^{3}, effectively. We prove the following.

Theorem 1.

There is an absolute effectively computable A>0A>0 such that

(1.1) log⁡c<A⁢R1/3⁢exp⁡(−log⁡R3000⁢log⁡log⁡R)\log c<AR^{1/3}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\log R}{3000\log\log R}\right)
